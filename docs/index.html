Core Architecture

Everything you love about Apple Music, powered by YouTube.

Engineered from the ground up on Deja's native Android/Desktop InnerTube audio pipeline.

Native Audio Engine

Direct 160kbps Opus audio stream resolution without video decoding overhead, built on Deja's native InnerTube architecture.

Stats for Nerds Telemetry

Real-time audio telemetry tracking bitrate, Opus buffer health, loudness normalization, audio pipeline latency, and dropped frames.

5-Provider Word-Synced Lyrics

SimpMusic RichSync, BetterLyrics TTML, LyricsPlus, LRCLIB, and YouTube timed captions with 60fps monotonic clock reconciliation.

5-Band Studio Equalizer

Hardware-accelerated Web Audio parametric equalizer with presets for Bass Boost, Acoustic, Vocal Booster, Treble Booster, and Flat profiles.

Sleep Timer with Fadeout

Automatic bedtime sleep timer with progressive acoustic volume fadeout for smooth, uninterrupted night-time listening.

Dynamic 4-Color Mesh Aura

GPU-rendered liquid mesh gradient derived dynamically from current album art palette colors, providing an immersive ambient glow.

1-Click Browser Sync with PNA

Instant session transfer using Private Network Access (PNA). Safely imports your Google session from Chrome/Brave/Edge without login blocks.

Multi-Account Profile Switcher

Seamlessly switch between personal, brand, and family YouTube Music channels directly from the titlebar account menu.

Personal Library & Playlist Sync

Multi-feed synchronization for your personal library, liked songs, albums, and custom playlists with continuation token pagination.

Discord Rich Presence

Live Discord presence broadcasting track titles, artist names, high-resolution album art, and real-time playback timestamps.

Google TOS & Ad Policy Compliance

Strict platform compliance: monetization ads are supported for free tiers, while YouTube Premium members enjoy native ad-free playback.

Interactive Lyrics

Sing along with 60fps word-by-word precision & offset calibration.

Deja features a 5-provider word-synced lyrics engine racing SimpMusic RichSync, BetterLyrics TTML, LyricsPlus, LRCLIB Enhanced LRC, and YouTube timed captions. Includes 60fps monotonic clock reconciliation, duet alignment, background vocals, and per-track offset calibration.

  • 5-provider word-synced engine: SimpMusic RichSync, BetterLyrics TTML, LyricsPlus, LRCLIB, and YouTube captions.
  • Timing offset nudge controls (−0.1s / +0.1s, −0.5s / +0.5s) stored per-track in localStorage.
  • Click any lyric line to jump playback directly to that moment with calibrated timing.
  • Frosted glass depth-of-field blur with glowing active line highlight.
