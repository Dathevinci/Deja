Core Architecture

Everything you love about Apple Music, powered by YouTube.

Engineered from the ground up on BitChord's native Android/Desktop InnerTube reverse-engineering pipeline.

BitChord Sign-In Engine

Untouched Google authentication with zero Botguard traps. Probes live ytcfg variables and lets you easily switch between personal and brand artist channels before confirming.

Dynamic Mesh Gradient Aura

Real-time palette extraction samples colors from album covers to render smooth liquid ambient lighting across the entire player backdrop with hardware acceleration.

160kbps Opus Audio Stream

Direct audio stream resolution with Stats for Nerds telemetry, extracting pure Opus audio at 160kbps for audiophile clarity with zero video decoding overhead.

Synced Lyrics via LRCLIB

Millisecond-accurate word and line synchronized lyrics powered by the LRCLIB open database. Features frosted blur focus and interactive tap-to-seek playback.

5-Band Studio Equalizer

Built-in Web Audio API parametric equalizer with presets for Bass Boost, Acoustic, Vocal Booster, and Treble Booster, plus custom gain tweaking.

Sleep Timer & Discord RPC

Gentle audio fade-out sleep timer so you can drift off to sleep, paired with rich Discord Presence broadcasting track name, artist, and live album art across IPC pipes.

Interactive Lyrics

Sing along with millisecond precision.

Deja queries LRCLIB using sanitized song metadata to provide time-synced lyrics with beautiful glowing active lines, gentle depth-of-field background blurs, and interactive click-to-seek.

  • ✓ Click any lyric line to jump playback directly to that moment.
